# Safety and Usage Notice

**SunnyNet is an experimental research project, not a product, and has not undergone any safety alignment process.**

## 1. General limitations

- The model is a small-scale prototype trained from scratch with limited training data; its output **is not guaranteed to be factually
  correct**, and carries risks of fabricated information, repetition, grammatical errors, logical contradictions, and harmful content.
- The model **has not undergone instruction tuning or alignment with human feedback**, has no reliable instruction-following capability,
  and cannot serve as a basis for any production or decision-making system.
- Any consequences arising from the use of model output are borne by the user.

## 2. Medical matters (important)

- This repository contains corpus in the domain of "classical Chinese medicine" (sourced from public-domain Chinese classical literature
  on Project Gutenberg). **These texts were written hundreds or even thousands of years ago, and the medical statements in them have not
  been verified by modern medicine.**
- The model's output on Chinese-medicine-related text is **only a linguistic result of language modeling, and in no way constitutes
  medical advice, a basis for diagnosis, or a basis for prescription**.
- **For any health problem, consult a physician with the appropriate license to practice. Do not use this model's output for
  self-diagnosis, self-medication, or any medical decision.**

## 3. Legal and compliance

- Users must themselves ensure that their use complies with the laws and regulations of their jurisdiction.
- This model must not be used to generate illegal content, false information, or content that infringes the rights of others.
- This model must not be used for any face / voiceprint / identity-related purpose without clearly informing the person concerned.

## 4. User obligations

Downloading or using any content in this repository means you have read, understood, and accepted all of the limitations above.
